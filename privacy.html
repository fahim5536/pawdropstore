At PAWDROP, the integrity, confidentiality, and security of our global pet owner community’s personal and relational data are our foremost priorities. This Privacy Policy documents our rigorous procedures regarding the acquisition, protection, and systematic deployment of your information across our web applications, checkout models, and automated customer assistance frameworks (such as PAWDROP AI Companion).

1. DATA ACQUISITION & INTENT

To process dropshipped orders, perform real-time checkouts, and deliver contextual veterinary diagnostic help, PAWDROP records specific parameters provided by you:

  • Identity Identifiers: Your full name, delivery destination, postal code, billing address, telephone numbers, and email accounts.
  • Profile Parameters: Registered pet names, breeds, birth metrics, symptom records, dietary configurations, and product favorites managed via our interface nodes.
  • Operational Telemetry: IP coordinates, browser system characteristics, local time records, session flows, and shopping interaction timings.

2. COHERENT COOKIE & STORAGE MECHANICS

PAWDROP deploys standard tracking coordinates, LocalStorage variables, and secure session state cookies to optimize e-commerce delivery flows. This includes:

  • Persistence Cookies: Retaining security tokens, items active inside your Shopping Cart, item additions in your Wishlist, and customized site interface settings (such as persistent color theme selections or localized currency settings).
  • Performance Metrics: Standard analytical identifiers mapping transaction pipelines to prevent broken pages, visual interface flashes, or repeated authorization prompts.

3. SECURITY SAFEGUARDS & GATEWAYS

We deploy high-level protection protocols to guard customer profiles:

  • All transaction gateways process checkout records using 256-bit Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) encryption protocols. Direct credit/debit card numbers are parsed instantly by authenticated standard processing clearinghouses and are never cached or housed on our servers.
  • Database queries interact with Cloud Firestore configured with strong write/read permissions, ensuring that arbitrary queries cannot extract private owner profiles.

4. RESTRICTED LOGISTICS TRANSFERS

To safely execute the dropshipping cycle, PAWDROP shares specific address fields and product identifiers exclusively with our verified direct production hubs, logistics clearinghouses, and supply networks (such as CJ Dropshipping integration routines). We strictly forbid these logistics agents from retaining your address for non-delivery or advertising endeavors. We never rent or barter your metadata to external marketing brokers.

5. YOUR CONSUMER RIGHTS & CHOICES

Under international guidelines, you maintain absolute control over your digital trace. You can issue requests to support@pawdrop.com for full visual access to your saved profile details, request immediate data rectification, or require the permanent deletion of your profile history from our active records.

At PAWDROP, we choose performance goods to ensure standard-setting utility and comfort for your pets. Because we operate an optimized direct-fulfillment dropshipping framework, our product return and customer reimbursement guidelines are strictly structured as follows:

1. DEFECTIVE PARCELS & ACCIDENTAL DAMAGES

If you receive a product that is damaged during transit, functionally defective, or different from the item ordered, you are protected by our direct warranty policy:

  • You must submit a formal refund or replacement claim by contacting support@pawdrop.com within 7 days of delivery.
  • Your claim email must include clear, high-resolution photographs and/or a video showing the defective product, the specific damage area, and the shipping parcel label.
  • Upon verification, PAWDROP will, at our sole cost, dispatch a brand new replacement product or issue a 100% financial refund. You are not required to return damaged goods in this instance.

2. STANDARD RETURN ELIGIBILITY

If you change your mind regarding a purchase, you may request a standard return within 14 days of receipt of the package, subject to the following severe constraints:

  • The pet gear must be in pristine, unused condition, free of pet hair, scent, or signs of wear, and complete with all original tags attached.
  • All items must be repacked inside their complete original manufacturing packaging.
  • Crucial: Product returns sent directly back to the manufacturer address, supplier coordinates, or global depot printed on the shipping container label without formal authorization from PAWDROP Support will not be processed, and no money refunds shall be issued.

3. PROCESS FOR AUTHORIZED RETURNS

To initiate a standard eligible return, you must follow these sequential steps:

  1. Contact Support at support@pawdrop.com stating your Order ID, email address, and reason for return.
  2. Once approved, our team will supply you with our localized PAWDROP returns depot address and a Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) ticket.
  3. Package the unused merchandise securely and ship it to the designated returns depot. The customer retains full responsibility for the shipping costs of standard returns; we do not provide prepaid shipping labels for buyer's-remorse situations. We strongly encourage shipping with tracking.

4. REVIEW TIMELINES & CREDITS

Upon receipt of the returned item at our returns depot, our warehouse team will inspect the items to verify if they meet our condition standards. This review lasts up to 5 business days. Once approved, we will issue a transaction credit to your original payment platform. Please note that credit card institutions and payment networks usually take 5 to 10 business days to clear funds back to your statement.
